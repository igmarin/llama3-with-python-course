# Shipping

Standard shipping takes 4 to 6 business days and costs $29 for bikes. Orders over $1,500 ship free. Accessories ship in 2 to 3 business days for a flat $6.

Express shipping delivers bikes in 2 business days for $79. Orders placed before 1 PM Eastern ship the same day. We do not ship bikes to PO boxes or outside the United States and Canada.

Every bike ships 90 percent assembled. You only need to attach the front wheel, handlebars, and pedals, which takes about 20 minutes with the included tools.
