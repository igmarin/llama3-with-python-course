# Helmet sizing guide

Measure your head with a soft tape about 2 cm above your eyebrows. A measurement of 52 to 56 cm is a small, 55 to 59 cm is a medium, and 58 to 62 cm is a large.

A helmet should sit level on your head, with two fingers of space between your eyebrow and the front rim. The side straps form a V just below each ear, and you should fit only one finger between the chin strap and your chin.

Replace any helmet after a crash, even if it looks fine, and replace it after five years of use.
