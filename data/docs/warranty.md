# Warranty

All Northwind frames carry a lifetime warranty against manufacturing defects for the original owner. Components such as the drivetrain, brakes, and wheels are covered for 2 years.

The warranty does not cover normal wear, such as worn brake pads, chains, and tires. It also does not cover damage from crashes, from racing, or from carrying more than 120 kg of rider and cargo.

To make a claim, email warranty@northwind.example with your order number and photos of the problem. We reply within 3 business days and either repair the bike, send replacement parts, or replace the frame.
