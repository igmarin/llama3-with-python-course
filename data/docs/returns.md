# Returns and refunds

You can return any bike within 30 days of delivery if it is unused and in its original packaging. Accessories such as helmets and locks can be returned within 14 days.

To start a return, open your order page and choose "Return item". We email a prepaid shipping label within one business day. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after the warehouse receives the item.

Custom-built bikes and items marked "final sale" cannot be returned. If a bike arrives damaged, contact support within 48 hours with photos and we will send a replacement at no cost.
