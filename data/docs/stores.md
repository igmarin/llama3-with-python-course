# Stores and service

Our flagship store is at 410 Harbor Street, Portland. It is open Monday to Friday from 10 AM to 7 PM, Saturday from 9 AM to 6 PM, and closed on Sunday.

The service desk offers a basic tune-up for $85, which includes brake and gear adjustment, tire pressure, and a chain check. A full overhaul costs $240 and takes 3 to 5 days. Tune-ups are usually done the same day if you arrive before noon.

You can book a free 30-minute fitting session online. A fitter measures your height and inseam and adjusts the saddle and handlebars to suit you.
